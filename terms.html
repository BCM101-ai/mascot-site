Publishing a pack

This is the part that matters if you draw one.

You keep your art

Submitting a pack does not give me ownership of anything. You keep the copyright, and you are free to do whatever else you like with the same artwork — sell it elsewhere, put it in a portfolio, give it away.

What you give me is permission to distribute it through Mascot: to store it, show it in the store, and let people install it. That permission is non-exclusive, and it lasts only while the pack is listed. Take the pack down and the permission ends with it, except that people who already installed it keep the copy they have — I cannot reach into anyone's Mac to remove it, and would not want to be able to.

What you are confirming

By submitting a pack you are telling me, and I am relying on it, that:

  • you made the artwork, or otherwise have the right to give it away;
  • it is not somebody else's character, logo or design — fan art of a character you do not own is the most common reason a pack is turned down;
  • it contains nothing sexual, nothing hateful, and nothing illegal;
  • it does not attempt to mislead the people installing it.

Review, and removal

Every pack is looked at by a person before it is listed. I may decline one, and I will tell you why. I may also remove a pack that is already listed if a problem comes to light — a copyright complaint, most likely — and I will tell you that too.

You can withdraw your own pack whenever you want, by asking. There is no notice period and you do not have to give a reason.
