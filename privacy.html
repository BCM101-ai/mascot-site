Privacy Policy

Mascot for macOS · Last updated 29 August 2026

The short version

Your coursework is read on your Mac, stored on your Mac, and never sent to me or to anyone else. There is no analytics and no tracking of any kind. Two parts of Mascot use the internet, and neither touches your coursework: the mascot store, which downloads a list of packs when you open it, and an optional account, which exists only so that someone who draws a mascot can publish it. You never need an account to use Mascot.

Who I am

Mascot is a macOS application made by one person, Thanachot Ananritkul. There is no company and no team, so this page says "I" rather than "we". You can reach me at Thanachot10072550@gmail.com.

What Mascot accesses

Only what you connect. Each source is optional and off until you enable it.

Source What is accessed Why
Google Classroom Your active courses, their coursework and due dates, and the status of your own submissions. Read-only. To show your deadlines, and to detect work you have already turned in so Mascot stops reminding you about it.
Apple Calendar Events in the single calendar you choose. To show deadlines you keep in your calendar.
Apple Reminders Reminders in the single list you choose, including whether each is completed. To show deadlines you keep in Reminders, and to stay quiet once you tick one off.
Deadlines you type in The title, due date and any link you enter yourself. To remind you about them.

Google account data specifically

Mascot requests three Google OAuth scopes, all of them read-only:

ScopeUsed for
classroom.courses.readonly Listing the courses you are enrolled in.
classroom.coursework.me.readonly Reading the coursework assigned to you and its due dates.
classroom.student-submissions.me.readonly Checking whether you have turned a piece of work in, so a finished assignment stops driving reminders.

Mascot cannot modify, submit, or delete anything in Google Classroom. It has no write access. It cannot see other students' work.

Where your data is stored

On your Mac, apart from two things, and both only if you choose to publish a mascot pack: the email address for your account, and any pack you submit. Nothing to do with your coursework is among them.

What leaves your Mac

Mascot connects to three places, and only for the reasons below. Everything else stays on your Mac.

Google, when you connect Classroom

These requests go directly from your Mac to Google's servers:

  • accounts.google.com — signing in
  • oauth2.googleapis.com — renewing your session
  • classroom.googleapis.com and www.googleapis.com — reading your coursework

The mascot store, when you open it

Mascot has a store where you can install mascot characters other people have drawn. Opening it downloads a list of available packs, and installing one downloads that pack. Both are ordinary files on a website:

  • studymascot.com — the list of packs, and the packs themselves

This happens only when you open the Store, never when the app starts. The request carries nothing about you: no account, no identifier, no coursework, nothing about your deadlines. Nothing is uploaded. GitHub, which serves that site, sees the request in the same way it sees anyone loading a web page; I am not given access to those logs.

My server, if you publish a pack

An account exists for one reason: so that a person who draws a mascot pack can publish it, be credited for it, and later be paid for it. You do not need one to use Mascot. Deadlines, the mascot, reminders, the store and installing packs all work signed out, and always will.

If you choose to make one, signing in sends to my account server:

  • your email address
  • your password, once, over an encrypted connection, to check it

The server keeps your email address and a scrambled form of your password that cannot be turned back into it. Your Mac keeps only the sign-in token it gets back, in the Keychain — never the password itself.

Your coursework is not part of this. It is never sent to the account server, or anywhere else, whether you have an account or not.

You can delete your account at any time by writing to me, and everything held for it goes with it.

Packs you submit, if you make one

This applies only to people who draw a mascot pack and send it in. If you are using Mascot to keep track of coursework, none of it touches you.

Submitting a pack uploads its artwork and a preview image to my server, along with the name, description and creator name you typed. They are stored privately: nobody but you and I can see a pack while it is waiting, which is the point of it being reviewed before anyone can install it.

  • If it is accepted, the pack becomes public in the store — that is what submitting it asks for.
  • If it is not accepted, it stays private, and you get a reason.
  • Either way you can ask me to delete it, and it goes.

Your coursework is not part of a submission any more than it is part of anything else. Only the files you chose and the words you typed are sent.

What I still do not receive

Your coursework, your calendar, your deadlines, or any record of how you use the app — with or without an account. There is nowhere on my side for any of it to go.

What I do not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is no data on my side to sell.
  • No use of your data to train machine-learning models.
  • No third-party SDKs.

Google API Services Limited Use

Mascot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: data obtained through Google APIs is used solely to provide the reminder features described on this site, is never transferred to any third party, is never used for advertising, and is never used to train models.

Your control

Disconnecting Google

Right-click the mascot and choose Sign Out of Classroom. This deletes the stored token from your Keychain immediately. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Deleting everything

Delete the Mascot app and remove its folder from ~/Library/Application Support/Mascot. That is everything held on your Mac. If you never made an account, that is genuinely all of it — there is nothing on my side to close and nothing to request from me. If you did make one, write to me and I will delete it and everything held for it.

Permissions

Calendar, Reminders and Accessibility access are granted by you in System Settings and can be withdrawn there at any time. Mascot continues to work without them, with the corresponding features switched off.

Children

Mascot is a study tool and may be used by students under 18. Used as a study tool it collects nothing about anyone and transmits nothing to me, regardless of age — and that is the whole app: no account is needed for any of it. An account is only for publishing a mascot pack, and if you are under 18 you should ask a parent before making one. If you are a parent or school with a question, please write to me.

Changes to this policy

If Mascot ever changes how it handles data, I will update this page and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app.
