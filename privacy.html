Privacy Policy

Mascot for macOS · Last updated 28 August 2026

The short version

Mascot has no servers. Your coursework is read on your Mac, stored on your Mac, and never sent to the developer or to anyone else. There is no account, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind.

Who we are

Mascot is a macOS application developed by Thanachot Ananritkul ("we", "us"). You can reach us at Thanachot10072550@gmail.com.

What Mascot accesses

Only what you connect. Each source is optional and off until you enable it.

Source What is accessed Why
Google Classroom Your active courses, their coursework and due dates, and the status of your own submissions. Read-only. To show your deadlines, and to detect work you have already turned in so Mascot stops reminding you about it.
Apple Calendar Events in the single calendar you choose. To show deadlines you keep in your calendar.
Apple Reminders Reminders in the single list you choose, including whether each is completed. To show deadlines you keep in Reminders, and to stay quiet once you tick one off.
Deadlines you type in The title, due date and any link you enter yourself. To remind you about them.

Google account data specifically

Mascot requests three Google OAuth scopes, all of them read-only:

ScopeUsed for
classroom.courses.readonly Listing the courses you are enrolled in.
classroom.coursework.me.readonly Reading the coursework assigned to you and its due dates.
classroom.student-submissions.me.readonly Checking whether you have turned a piece of work in, so a finished assignment stops driving reminders.

Mascot cannot modify, submit, or delete anything in Google Classroom. It has no write access. It cannot see other students' work.

Where your data is stored

On your Mac. Nowhere else.

WhatWhere
Your Google sign-in token The macOS Keychain, encrypted by the operating system.
Deadlines you typed in yourself A file in your Application Support folder.
Preferences — which calendar you chose,
what you have ticked off, where the mascot sits
 Standard macOS preferences on your Mac.
Coursework from Google Classroom, Calendar and Reminders Held in memory while the app is running and discarded when it quits. It is not written to disk.

What leaves your Mac

Mascot connects to Google, and to nothing else. Those requests go directly from your Mac to Google's servers:

  • accounts.google.com — signing in
  • oauth2.googleapis.com — renewing your session
  • classroom.googleapis.com and www.googleapis.com — reading your coursework

There is no Mascot server. We do not receive your coursework, your calendar, your email address, your name, or any usage data — there is nothing on our side to receive it.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of data. There is no data on our side to sell.
  • No use of your data to train machine-learning models.
  • No third-party SDKs.

Google API Services Limited Use

Mascot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: data obtained through Google APIs is used solely to provide the reminder features described on this site, is never transferred to any third party, is never used for advertising, and is never used to train models.

Your control

Disconnecting Google

Right-click the mascot and choose Sign Out of Classroom. This deletes the stored token from your Keychain immediately. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Deleting everything

Delete the Mascot app and remove its folder from ~/Library/Application Support/Mascot. Because nothing is stored anywhere else, that is all of it. There is no account to close and nothing to request from us.

Permissions

Calendar, Reminders and Accessibility access are granted by you in System Settings and can be withdrawn there at any time. Mascot continues to work without them, with the corresponding features switched off.

Children

Mascot is a study tool and may be used by students under 18. It collects nothing about anyone, and it transmits nothing to us, regardless of age. If you are a parent or school with a question, please write to us.

Changes to this policy

If Mascot ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will also be noted in the app.
