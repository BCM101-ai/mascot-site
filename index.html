Make your own

The capybara is just the first one. Every mood is its own drawing with its own frames, so a pack can have calm asleep in one frame and overdue on fire in eight.

  1. In Mascot, turn on Settings › Making packs › Show creator tools.
  2. Drop a drawing into each mood slot and confirm the frame count.
  3. Install and wear it to judge it on your own desk, then Export for submission.

Every pack is looked at by a person before it is listed, most obviously for art that is not the submitter's to share.

The format, in one breath

PNG with alpha. Frames left‑to‑right in one row. Ten frames a second, always — urgency is yours to draw, not ours to fake. 4–8 frames per mood is the sweet spot.

Questions

The ones people ask.

Does it work with Microsoft Teams?

Not automatically. Teams Assignments cannot be read by third-party apps without your school's IT department approving it. Instead, copy an assignment's text and paste it into Mascot's quick-add box — it will work out the title and due date.

My school blocks it

Some schools block third-party apps from connecting to Google accounts. If sign-in fails, your IT administrator would need to allow Mascot. Everything else in the app still works without it.
