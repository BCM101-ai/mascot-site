A study buddy that lives on your Mac.

Mascot sits on your Dock and keeps an eye on your coursework. When a deadline gets close it says so — before you find out the hard way.

Download for macOS

What it does

Read

Sign in once and your coursework appears automatically. Mascot also sees what you have already turned in, so it stops reminding you about work that is done.

Reacts

The capybara is relaxed when you have time and increasingly less relaxed when you do not. You learn how much trouble you are in at a glance, without opening anything.

Add

Press ⇧Space anywhere and type essay friday 5pm. Mascot works out the title and the date. Useful for coursework that never made it to Classroom — Teams assignments, group projects, things a lecturer only said out loud.

Syncs

Add a deadline on your phone and it shows up on your Mac. Tick it off there and Mascot goes quiet.

Your coursework stays on your Mac

Mascot has no servers. Nothing you do in it is sent to us, because there is no "us" for it to be sent to — your Mac talks directly to Google and nowhere else.

No account to create. No analytics. No tracking. Your coursework is never transmitted to the developer or to any third party. The full details are in the privacy policy.

Download

Download Mascot

Questions

Does it work with Microsoft Teams?

Not automatically. Teams Assignments cannot be read by third-party apps without your school's IT department approving it. Instead, copy an assignment's text and paste it into Mascot's quick-add box — it will work out the title and due date.

My school blocks it

Some schools block third-party apps from connecting to Google accounts. If sign-in fails, your IT administrator would need to allow Mascot. Everything else in the app still works without it.
